Section 02 // Epidemiological Rigor

The "15.8% Case Fatality Rate" Paradox Explained

Why Algeria's early pandemic indicators falsely suggested one of the deadliest outbreaks globally, and how diagnostic rationing skewed official metrics.

Methodological Post-Mortem: Denominator Ascertainment Bias

In April 2020, international media reported Algeria’s Case Fatality Rate (CFR) at 15.8%, higher than Italy (13.1%) and Spain (10.5%). As a quantitative analyst, examining this metric reveals an extreme ascertainment artifact rather than hyper-lethal clinical virulence. The Pasteur Institute of Algeria initially had a centralized national capacity of only ~50–100 RT-PCR kits per day for a population of 44 million. Consequently, PCR testing was strictly triaged for end-stage hospitalized patients with acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS). Because the thousands of mild and asymptomatic community transmissions were never sampled or logged, the official statistical denominator was artificially suppressed by a factor of 8x–12x.

Frontline Protocol: Chest CT Scans as PCR Proxy

To overcome week-long PCR backlogs, the Algerian Scientific Committee officially authorized thoracic Low-Dose CT (Computed Tomography) scans for diagnostic triage based on bilateral ground-glass opacities.

Section 05 // Governance & Chronology

Critical Intervention Milestones

A granular timeline of non-pharmaceutical interventions, containment mandates, and political developments throughout 2020.

FEBRUARY 25, 2020

First Confirmed Case Detected in Ouargla

An Italian petroleum engineer operating in Hassi Messaoud tested positive at the Pasteur Institute, marking the official index case in Algeria. Rapid contact isolation was attempted in southern desert bases.

MARCH 20, 2020

Hirak Movement Voluntarily Suspends Mass Rallies

Following 56 consecutive weeks of peaceful anti-establishment protests across major cities, civic organizers and medical committees called for a voluntary suspension of all street marches to safeguard public health.

MARCH 24, 2020

"Variable Geometry" Lockdown & Blida Military Cordon

Blida Province was placed under total 24-hour quarantine with security checkpoints sealing all access roads. Algiers and surrounding wilayas were placed under an overnight 7 PM – 7 AM curfew. All schools, universities, and mosques were shuttered.

APRIL 15, 2020

National CFR Peaks at 15.8% Amid Testing Deficit

Official data registered 336 deaths against 2,160 confirmed cases. With PCR testing restricted to severe hospitalizations, the mortality ratio peaked, driving authorities to approve chest CT scans and begin decentralizing testing to regional universities.

MAY 24, 2020

Mandatory Mask Decrees & Eid Containment Measures

Executive decree required wearing masks in all public spaces, markets, and public transit, accompanied by strict fines. Inter-wilaya public transport was halted over the Eid holiday weekend.

JULY 24, 2020

Wave 2 Climax (675 Daily Cases) & Frontline Mobilization

Second wave peaked following economic reopening. Acute shortages of oxygen concentrators in eastern provinces prompted widespread diaspora fundraising initiatives and grassroots oxygen volunteer networks.

NOVEMBER 24, 2020

Wave 3 Zenith (1,133 Cases) & Curfew Re-escalation

The highest single-day official count of the year was recorded. The government re-introduced 8 PM curfews across 32 provinces, shut gymnasiums and cultural halls, and banned all gatherings exceeding 10 individuals.

Section 06 // Data Engineering & Pipeline

ETL Architecture & Reproducibility

Python-based data extraction, cleaning transformations, rolling-window calculations, and outlier normalization pipeline.

pipeline/clean_algeria_covid2020.py PYTHON 3.11 
# ==============================================================================
# Algeria COVID-19 (2020) Quantitative Data Processing Pipeline
# Author: Salaheddine Kennouda
# ==============================================================================
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

def process_epidemiological_time_series(raw_bulletin_path: str) -> pd.DataFrame:
    # Ingest daily official MSPRH communiqués and normalize dates
    df = pd.read_csv(raw_bulletin_path, parse_dates=["date"])
    df = df.sort_values("date").reset_index(drop=True)
    
    # Handle batch reporting anomalies and retrospective adjustments
    df["daily_cases_cleaned"] = df["daily_cases"].clip(lower=0)
    
    # Compute 7-Day Centered Moving Average to remove weekend reporting lag
    df["sma_7d"] = (
        df["daily_cases_cleaned"]
        .rolling(window=7, min_periods=1, center=True)
        .mean()
        .round(1)
    )
    
    # Case Fatality Rate (CFR) trajectory computation
    df["cumulative_cases"] = df["daily_cases_cleaned"].cumsum()
    df["cumulative_deaths"] = df["daily_deaths"].cumsum()
    df["crude_cfr"] = (df["cumulative_deaths"] / df["cumulative_cases"]) * 100
    
    return df
